Can I use Adaure Argan Oil on relaxed, natural, or loc'd hair?
Yes! Adaure Moroccan Argan Hair Oil is formulated for ALL hair types — natural afro-textured hair, relaxed hair, loc'd hair, braids, and even weaves. The ultra-lightweight formula absorbs without leaving buildup or residue on any hair type or texture.

Adaure Argan Oil is a leave-in treatment. Apply a small amount to your scalp and hair, massage for 1 minute, and style as normal. You do NOT need to wash it out. Use twice daily — once in the morning and once before bed — for best results. The lightweight formula won't make your hair greasy.

Absolutely! Adaure Argan Oil works equally well for men and women. Men experiencing pattern baldness, receding hairlines, or general thinning have reported excellent results. The herbal formula stimulates follicles that may be dormant — making it effective even for areas with significant hair loss.
